The company reported steady results for the third quarter. Revenue grew by six percent compared with the same period last year, driven by strong demand in the northern region. Operating costs rose because of higher shipping expenses and a delay in opening the new warehouse. Management said the delay is a temporary setback and expects the site to open next quarter. The board approved a plan to hire forty more staff in customer support. Analysts remain cautious about rising costs and uncertain consumer spending. The chief executive thanked employees for their hard work and said the outlook is stable. The company will publish its full report on the first of next month.
