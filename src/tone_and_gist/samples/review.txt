I bought this blender three months ago and I love it. It crushes ice without any fuss and the smoothies come out perfectly smooth. Cleaning is simple because the jar goes straight into the dishwasher. The motor is loud, though, and the lid was hard to remove at first. My partner thinks the design looks beautiful on our counter. The price felt high when I ordered it, but I now think it was worth every penny. Delivery was quick and the packaging was secure. I would happily recommend it to anyone who makes smoothies regularly.
